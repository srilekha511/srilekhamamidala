The Sunflower Spiral

Every seed sits at the golden angle (137.508°) from the one before, and a fixed fraction closer to the centre. That makes the seed head mathematically self-similar: zoom in by one seed and the picture is identical, so it can go on forever with no cuts. As you go deeper, the head drifts through different flowers, and every so often a seed blooms into a small flower carrying a piece of the site. Click one to open it.
