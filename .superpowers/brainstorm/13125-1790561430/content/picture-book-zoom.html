A picture book that never ends

A landscape with a cottage. Zoom into its window, then the room, then the desk, where the open notebook is your About page. The sketch on the facing page turns out to be a whole new landscape: Selected Works. It keeps going, with the light getting later on each loop and each new desk holding one of your projects. Every scene is painted in watercolor by code.
